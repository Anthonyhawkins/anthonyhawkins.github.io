About

About This Site

Orbital Protocol is a personal learning journal documenting my journey from terrestrial networking (TCP, IP, gRPC, edge protocols) into the world of satellite communications and space networking.

Who I Am

I’m a lead software engineer specializing in TCP-based networking, IP, gRPC, and edge protocols, currently working in the payments industry. I’m betting that the commercialization of satellite constellations — driven by SpaceX Starlink, Amazon Kuiper, and others — is one of the most significant infrastructure shifts of the next decade.

I’m building the knowledge to be part of it.

What This Site Documents

  • Daily journal — what I’m studying, building, and figuring out
  • The Curriculum — a 52-week parallel-track study plan spanning math (Algebra 2 → Calculus → Linear Algebra → Probability → Graph Theory), C++ systems programming, and space networking protocols
  • Projects — hands-on implementations in C++ and Python, from CCSDS packet parsers to DTN bundle forwarders to LEO constellation simulators

The Goal

To transition into engineering roles focused on how Earth communicates with satellites, how satellites communicate with each other, and how to optimize those networks at scale.

The space industry needs people who can think about networking at scale — not just “how does one satellite talk to ground” but “how do 6,000 satellites form a coherent, self-healing, low-latency global network.”

That’s the goal.
